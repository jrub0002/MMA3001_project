# MMA3001_project
In this project, I have chosen to explore how machine learning techniques can be used to automate food packaging quality inspection processes. Packaging defects in commercial food retail can cause waste, consumer complaints, and recalls, whilst unsealed packaging may also affect food safety and shelf life. Manual inspection of food packaging can be slow, inconsistent, and fatiguing, limiting its ability to scale with the growing rate of production line speeds. Therefore, implementation of automated detection models could flag defective trays before they are distributed, minimising the negative impacts of packaging errors and reducing human effort and error. 
This project aims to narrow the scope of this issue by comparing two detection methods, YOLOv8n and Faster R-CNN ResNet-50 FPN detection models, to uncover the strengths and weaknesses of each model to determine which option offers the best trade-offs for real-time inspection. The factors which will be considered are accuracy, speed, and resource use of each model.
